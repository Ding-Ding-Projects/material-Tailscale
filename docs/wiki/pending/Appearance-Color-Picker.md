# Infinite colour picker

## Behaviour

A saturation and brightness field, hue and opacity sliders, and typed entry in any CSS colour. A translator shows the nearest name, HEX, HEX8, RGB, HSL, HSV, HWB, CIELAB, LCH, OKLab, OKLCH and CMYK with copy, contrast against white and black, and the animated rainbow option.

## Configuration

Used for the seed colour and every colour in the appearance editor.

## When something goes wrong

Colours outside sRGB warn before clipping.
