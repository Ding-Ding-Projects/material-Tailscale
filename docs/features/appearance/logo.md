# Logo presets and upload

## Behaviour

Three presets (Mesh, Orbit, Knot) and a local image upload that is decoded, size-checked, square-cropped and converted to PNG.

## Configuration

Settings > Appearance.

## When something goes wrong

Oversized or undecodable images are refused with the reason.

## Security and privacy

The image never leaves this computer.

## How to verify

Upload a logo and reset.
