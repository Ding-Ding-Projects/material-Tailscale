# Taildrive

## Behaviour

Lists folders shared with `tailscale drive list` and offers share (with a folder browser), rename and unshare.

## Configuration

Share names are plain text; paths come from the folder picker or drag and drop.

## When something goes wrong

Taildrive needs the drive node attributes in your policy; Tailscale's error says so.

## Security and privacy

Shared folders are reachable by tailnet members your policy allows.

## How to verify

Share a folder and confirm it appears in the list.
