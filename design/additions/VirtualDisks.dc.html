help

What each action does

  • Grow: raises the maximum while detached, then can extend the last partition.
  • Compact: frees empty blocks of a dynamic disk; the disk is attached read-only while it runs.
  • Convert: copies the disk into a new VHD or VHDX file; the original stays.
  • Merge: folds a differencing disk into its parent; other children of that parent stop working.
